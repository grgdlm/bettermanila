# Join trade fairs, business expos and tourism promotions

Manila runs bazaars, fairs and promotional events that local sellers can join. This page covers who to approach and where announcements appear.

---

## Who to approach

| Office                               | What it covers                              | Link                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Tourism and Cultural Affairs**     | City tourism events and promotions          | <https://manila.gov.ph/tourism/> |
| **Bureau of Permits**                | The business permit you need before trading | <https://manila.gov.ph/permits/> |
| **Public Employment Service Office** | Job fairs rather than trade fairs           | <https://manila.gov.ph/peso/>    |

---

## Where announcements appear

The city posts calls for participants and event dates on its news page rather than a standing directory:

- <https://manila.gov.ph/news/>

Recent examples the city has published include the One Ilocos Sur Bazaar at the Kartilya ng Katipunan, and Gawad Manileno, which honors top citizens and taxpayers.

## Before you apply

You will normally need a valid Manila business permit, so start there if you do not have one. See [applying for a business permit](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits).
