# Report illegal dumping or a waste violation

Illegal dumping is prohibited under national law and the city's waste department is explicitly tasked with enforcing it.

---

## What you can report

- Dumping waste in streets, waterways, esteros or vacant lots
- Putting waste out outside collection hours, which the city calls late dumping
- Burning waste, which is prohibited under RA 9003
- Mixing hazardous waste into regular collection
- Establishments failing to segregate

---

## Where to report

| Channel                           | Use it for                                                                                                | Link                                |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Bilis Kilos Action Center**     | The city's rapid response desk for resident concerns, supervised by the Office of the Mayor               | <https://manila.gov.ph/biliskilos/> |
| **Department of Public Services** | Waste specifically. (02) 8527 9638, Ground floor, Old Comelec Building, Lions Road, Arroceros St., Ermita | <https://manila.gov.ph/dps/>        |
| **Your barangay**                 | Local enforcement, and often the fastest for a specific address                                           | The barangay covering the location  |

The Department of Public Services lists implementing and enforcing all environment and solid waste management laws and ordinances among its core responsibilities.

---

## What helps your report

- The **exact location**, with a landmark or the nearest house number
- The **date and time** you saw it, and whether it repeats
- **Photos**, if you can take them safely
- Whether it is a one-off or an ongoing dumping spot

Do not confront anyone. Report it.
