# Educational support from the city

Manila runs schools, a city university, a public library and employment services. This page covers what exists and who to contact.

---

## The offices that run education in Manila

| Office                                      | What it covers                                                   | Contact                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Division of City Schools, Manila**        | Public elementary and secondary schools in the city, under DepEd | sdo.manila@deped.gov.ph, 8527 5180, 8527 2315, 8527 5184 |
| **Manila City Library**                     | Free study space and library services                            | <https://manila.gov.ph/citylibrary/>                     |
| **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** | Employment help, including student employment programs           | <https://manila.gov.ph/peso/>                            |
| **Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila (PLM)**  | The city's own university                                        | <https://manila.gov.ph/education/>                       |

---

## Programs the city has publicized

**YorMilk 2.0** is a city nutrition program for Manila children, continuing as of 2026.

The city is also building a **PLM College of Allied Health Sciences** building, and reports ongoing school construction such as Ramon Magsaysay High School.
