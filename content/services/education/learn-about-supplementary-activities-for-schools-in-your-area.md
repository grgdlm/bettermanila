# School activities and student programs

This page covers where to find out about student organizations, enrichment programs and school activities in Manila.

---

## Where to look first

| Source                               | What it gives you                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your child's school                  | The most reliable source. Clubs and organizations are organized per school  |
| **Division of City Schools, Manila** | sdo.manila@deped.gov.ph, 8527 5180, 8527 2315, 8527 5184                    |
| **City news page**                   | Activities, advisories and class suspensions: <https://manila.gov.ph/news/> |
| **Manila City Library**              | Study space and library programs: <https://manila.gov.ph/citylibrary/>      |

---

## Organizations recognized nationally

DepEd recognizes standard student organizations that exist across public schools nationwide, including the Supreme Student Government, the school paper, and scouting through the Boy Scouts and Girl Scouts of the Philippines.

Manila participates in scouting: the city joined the 63rd Scout Memorial Day.

---

## Alternative pathways

For learners who cannot attend regular classes, the **Alternative Learning System** is DepEd's national program. Ask the Division of City Schools which Manila schools host ALS sessions.
