# Apply for a scholarship

Manila runs its own university and coordinates with national scholarship programs. This page covers what is confirmed and who to ask.

---

## Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila

**PLM** is the City of Manila's own university. It is the most direct route to subsidized higher education for Manila residents.

## National programs open to Manila students

These are national, not city-run, and any Philippine student may apply:

| Program                                                           | Run by           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Tertiary Education Subsidy and free tuition in state universities | CHED and UniFAST |
| Technical and vocational scholarships                             | TESDA            |
| Student financial assistance programs                             | CHED             |

Applications and deadlines are set nationally. Check the agencies directly rather than through the city.
