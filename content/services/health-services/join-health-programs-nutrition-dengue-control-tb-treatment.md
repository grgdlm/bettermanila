## Disease surveillance

The city publishes a recurring **W.I.L.D. Diseases** report and update, covering water-borne, insect-borne, and other communicable disease activity in Manila.

Watch <https://manila.gov.ph/news/> for the current report. This is the city's own surveillance reporting, so it is the authoritative source for what is circulating in Manila right now.

## Free care generally

**Sta. Ana Hospital** provides all services free to Manila residents, and free to all senior citizens where available. **Ospital ng Tondo** provides free consultation, laboratory, X-ray, hospitalization and medicines.
