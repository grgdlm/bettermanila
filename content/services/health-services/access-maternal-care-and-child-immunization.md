# Maternal care and child immunization

This page covers where mothers and children get care in Manila.

---

## The hospital built around mother and child care

**Justice Jose Abad Santos General Hospital** in District III was originally established for mother and child programs before becoming a general hospital. It remains the city's strongest facility for this.

It was the **first local government hospital in the country** to earn the Mother-Baby Friendly Hospital Initiative accreditation, in 2013, recertified in August 2018.

Its relevant departments include Obstetrics and Gynecology, and Pediatrics with subspecialties in development, infectious disease, cardiology, allergology and endocrinology.

The hospital serves Binondo, Quiapo, San Nicolas and Santa Cruz, and states that it also accepts patients from neighboring districts.
