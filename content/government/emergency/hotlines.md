# Emergency hotlines for the City of Manila

Every emergency number the City of Manila publishes, as text you can tap, copy and search instead of an image you have to squint at. If you are not sure who to call, call 911 first.

---

## Call 911 first

**911** is the nationwide emergency hotline. It is free, it works from any phone, and it reaches police, fire and ambulance. Call it first, then call the station nearest you from the lists below if you can.

| If this is happening                             | Call                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anything urgent and you are not sure who to call | **911**                                                       |
| Fire                                             | **911**, then the nearest fire station below                  |
| Someone needs an ambulance                       | **911**, then an EMS unit below                               |
| Crime, violence, someone in danger               | **911**, then the nearest police station below                |
| Flood, storm, rescue, evacuation                 | **0932-662-2322** or **0950-700-3710** (city disaster office) |

Save 911 in your phone now, before you need it.

## A few things worth knowing

Some numbers here are printed by the city in the older seven digit format, such as 253-6808. They are reproduced exactly as published, with nothing added or corrected. If a seven digit number does not connect, try 911.

The city's hotline page does not list numbers for electricity, water or the barangay, so none are invented here.

The same city page also carries a list of open evacuation centers, but that list was published for a specific storm in November 2024 and is not a standing list. Check the [city's page](https://manila.gov.ph/emergency-police-fire-station-hotlines/) for the current one during an emergency.
