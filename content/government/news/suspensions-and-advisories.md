# Class suspensions, weather advisories and announcements

When a typhoon is coming, the question every household in Manila asks is whether there are classes. This page covers where the answer actually gets published.

---

## The official sources, in order

| Source                        | What it carries                                            | Link                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **City news page**            | Class suspensions, rainfall advisories, city announcements | <https://manila.gov.ph/news/>                                   |
| **Emergency hotlines**        | Police, fire and emergency numbers                         | <https://manila.gov.ph/emergency-police-fire-station-hotlines/> |
| **Bilis Kilos Action Center** | Reporting a problem to the city                            | <https://manila.gov.ph/biliskilos/>                             |

The city publishes suspension announcements directly, in the form **WALANG KLASE SA MAYNILA** followed by the date. It also issues a **City of Manila Localized Rainfall Advisory**, numbered per event.

---

## Who can suspend classes

Suspensions come from more than one authority, which is why announcements sometimes appear to conflict:

- **The Mayor** may suspend classes and government work in Manila.
- **DepEd** may suspend at division or national level.
- **Automatic suspension rules** apply at certain PAGASA storm signal levels.

If the city and a national announcement disagree, the city announcement governs Manila specifically.

---

## What else the city publishes

The city issues a recurring **W.I.L.D. Diseases** report covering communicable disease activity in Manila, and posts flood, fire and public works advisories through the same channel.

---

## A word on unofficial sources

Screenshots of suspension announcements circulate on Facebook and Viber faster than the city can publish, and a meaningful share of them are fabricated or recycled from a previous storm. Check the date on the announcement and confirm against the city's own page before acting on it.
