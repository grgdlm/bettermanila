# Procurement and bidding documents

Every peso the city spends on goods, services and infrastructure goes through procurement. Those documents are public.

---

## What the city publishes

The City of Manila's transparency page carries its Bids and Awards Committee documents:

| Document                                             | What it tells you                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invitation to Bid** and supplemental bid bulletins | What the city wants to buy or build, and the terms                  |
| **Request for Quotation**                            | Smaller purchases sought without full bidding                       |
| **Notice of Award**                                  | Who won, which is the document that matters most for accountability |

All of these are at <https://manila.gov.ph/transparency/>.

---

## The national portal

**PhilGEPS** is the central portal for all Philippine government procurement, and it publishes to the **Open Contracting Data Standard**.

- Open data: <https://open.philgeps.gov.ph/>

PhilGEPS is generally the better source if you want to compare Manila against other cities, or track a supplier across multiple contracts.

---

## Why the Notice of Award is the one to read

An invitation to bid tells you what was wanted. A notice of award tells you **who got the money**. If you are checking whether a project was awarded fairly, that is the document to start from, then work backwards to the bid terms.
